## Q1. Lineage of Samsung's modem interface drivers after modem_v1 (modem_if, cpif, Pixel Tensor), structure, modernisation, ABI continuity

### Takeaway
The driver family evolved modem_if (Exynos 4 era, 3.0-3.4) → modem_v1 (3.10–4.14; `link_device_lli.c` exists only in its 3.10 Exynos 5430/5433/7420 trees) → Samsung "cpif" (`drivers/soc/samsung/cpif`, 4.19–5.15) and Google's fork (`drivers/soc/google/cpif` on 5.10, then `google-modules/radio/samsung/s5300|s5400` on 6.1); none of the post-3.10 trees supports MIPI-LLI or SS333 — they target integrated ModAP (shmem + mailbox, SMC secure boot) or the external S5100/S5300/S5400 over PCIe. The userspace ABI is *family-compatible but not identical*: node names, SIPC5 channel numbers and most `_IO('o', n)` values survive, but cpif renamed ioctls, made 0x21/0x22 `_IOW(…, struct boot_mode)` (different numbers) and dropped the DL_START/FW_UPDATE/SET_TX_LINK/BOOT_DONE path our cbd uses. The mystery 0x6f47/0x6f48 are `IOCTL_MODEM_GET_CP_BOOTLOG` / `IOCTL_MODEM_CLR_CP_BOOTLOG`.

**What cpif modernised (vs our 3.10 modem_v1)**
- Wakeup sources instead of Android wakelocks: `cpif_wake_lock_register()` → `wakeup_source_register` (modem_main.c:252, link_device.c:4000, modem_ctrl_s5100.c:2235) — confirmed.
- Reserved memory from DT: `shm_ipc.c` walks `memory-region` phandles with `of_reserved_mem_lookup()` (shm_ipc.c:44-59) or, when built in, an early hook `RESERVEDMEM_OF_DECLARE(modem_if, "exynos,modem_if", cp_rmem_setup)` (:75-96) — confirmed, [shm_ipc.c](https://android.googlesource.com/kernel/google-modules/radio/samsung/s5300/+/refs/heads/android-gs-bluejay-android16/shm_ipc.c). But it still maps with `phys_to_virt()`/`vmap()` of struct pages (shm_ipc.c:216, 373, 389), which only works for mapped (not `no-map`) lowmem.
- NAPI + GRO: `netif_napi_add(&mld->dummy_net, &mld->mld_napi, mld_rx_int_poll)` (link_device.c:4169), per-queue NAPI in pktproc (link_rx_pktproc.c:2076), `napi_gro_receive` (modem_io_device.c:453) — confirmed. Our 3.10 SS333 config uses tasklets with `# CONFIG_LINK_DEVICE_NAPI is not set` (`SS333_LLI_BRINGUP.md` §1.1).
- DMA API for packet buffers (`dma_map_single_attrs`, `dma_sync_single_for_cpu/device` in link_rx_pktproc.c:192, 355, 476, 1963; link_device.c:1687-1692) but still `virt_to_phys()`/`phys_to_virt()` address arithmetic — confirmed.
- Character devices: Google's cpif replaced `misc_register` with `alloc_chrdev_region(…, "cpif")` + `class_create("cpif")` + `cdev_add` + `device_create(…, "%s", iod->name)` (modem_main.c:721-731, modem_io_device.c:664-690), so nodes keep their names (`/dev/umts_ipc0`…) under class `cpif` instead of `misc` — confirmed (5.10: modem_main.c:680-686, modem_io_device.c:694). Samsung's own 4.19 cpif still uses `misc_register(&iod->miscdev)` (modem_io_device.c:745,758 in [LineageOS exynos850](https://github.com/LineageOS/android_kernel_samsung_exynos850/blob/lineage-24.0/drivers/soc/samsung/cpif/modem_io_device.c)) — confirmed.
- rmnet netdevs still created by the driver with `alloc_netdev`/`alloc_netdev_mqs` + `register_netdev` (modem_io_device.c:730-770); no use of the wwan framework — confirmed.

**ABI continuity and the 0x6f47/0x6f48 puzzle**
- Samsung modem_v1 for Exynos 9810 (Linux 4.9, LineageOS lineage-20) keeps *every* value of our table (`IOCTL_MODEM_ON _IO('o',0x19)` … `IOCTL_LINK_DEVICE_RESET 0x44`) and adds `IOCTL_MODEM_GET_SHMEM_SRINFO 0x45`, `SET_SHMEM_SRINFO 0x46`, **`IOCTL_MODEM_GET_CP_BOOTLOG _IO('o', 0x47)`**, **`IOCTL_MODEM_CLR_CP_BOOTLOG _IO('o', 0x48)`**, `IOCTL_SECURITY_REQ 0x53` … `IOCTL_CPLOG_FULL_DUMP 0x59` — confirmed, [modem_prj.h:74-128](https://github.com/LineageOS/android_kernel_samsung_exynos9810/blob/lineage-20/virt/drivers/misc/modem_v1/modem_prj.h). Handler: `link_device_shmem.c:2603` `#define SHMEM_BOOTLOG_BASE 0xC00`; GET reads a u32 length + string at shmem+0xC00 and prints "CP boot log", CLR memsets it (:2667-2688) — confirmed, [link_device_shmem.c](https://github.com/LineageOS/android_kernel_samsung_exynos9810/blob/lineage-20/virt/drivers/misc/modem_v1/link_device_shmem.c). Same semantics in Google cpif `IOCTL_GET_CP_BOOTLOG/IOCTL_CLR_CP_BOOTLOG` (modem_prj.h:102-103; link_device.c:2778-2800; `SHMEM_BOOTLOG_BASE 0xC00`, `_BUFF 0x1FF`, `_OFFSET 0x4` in link_device_memory.h:110-112) — confirmed.
- That 9810 tree still defines the same `shmem_4mb_phys_map` with `SHM_4M_RESERVED_SZ 4056` and `MEM_IPC_MAGIC 0xAA` (link_device_memory.h:71,104,535) — confirmed; so offset 0xC00 lies inside the `reserved[4056]` hole of the same legacy map our SS333 uses.
- Google cpif (5.10 and 6.1) renames and partly renumbers: `IOCTL_POWER_ON _IO(0x19)`, `IOCTL_POWER_OFF _IO(0x20)`, **`IOCTL_POWER_RESET _IOW('o',0x21,struct boot_mode)`**, **`IOCTL_START_CP_BOOTLOADER _IOW('o',0x22,struct boot_mode)`** (`struct boot_mode { enum cp_boot_mode idx; }`), `IOCTL_COMPLETE_NORMAL_BOOTUP _IO(0x23)`, `IOCTL_GET_CP_STATUS 0x27`, `IOCTL_START_CP_DUMP 0x32`, `IOCTL_TRIGGER_CP_CRASH 0x34`, `IOCTL_TRIGGER_KERNEL_PANIC 0x35`, `IOCTL_LOAD_CP_IMAGE _IOW(0x40, struct cp_image)`, `IOCTL_GET_CP_CRASH_REASON _IOR(0x55)`, `IOCTL_GET_CPIF_VERSION _IOR(0x56)`, `IOCTL_SET_SPI_BOOT_MODE _IO(0x58)`, `IOCTL_GET_OPENED_STATUS _IOR(0x59,int)`, `IOCTL_SILENT_RESET _IO(0x60)` — confirmed, [GS-S5300 modem_prj.h:56-203](https://android.googlesource.com/kernel/google-modules/radio/samsung/s5300/+/refs/heads/android-gs-bluejay-android16/modem_prj.h); 5.10 identical for 0x19-0x57 ([kernel/gs modem_prj.h:61-188](https://android.googlesource.com/kernel/gs/+/refs/heads/android-gs-raviole-5.10-android12-d1/drivers/soc/google/cpif/modem_prj.h)). Samsung's 4.19 cpif has the same `_IOW` form (`IOCTL_START_CP_BOOTLOADER` at modem_prj.h:82 of LineageOS exynos850) — confirmed.
- The image-load model changed: cpif loads images with one `IOCTL_LOAD_CP_IMAGE {binary, size, m_offset, b_offset, mode, len}` (modem_prj.h:84-90) instead of our `DL_START` + `FW_UPDATE {size,mtu,num_frames}` + framed `write()` — confirmed; no `IOCTL_MODEM_SET_TX_LINK (0x37)`, `DL_START (0x28)`, `FW_UPDATE (0x29)` or `BOOT_DONE (0x24)` in cpif's table (same file) — confirmed.
- Google cpif has no SS333 or LLI support at all: no `link_device_lli`, controllers are only `modem_ctrl_s5000ap.c`/`modem_ctrl_s5100.c` (tree listings 5.10 and 6.1) — confirmed.

### Inferences
- For SS333 the **reference implementation stays our own 3.10 modem_v1**; cpif is useful as a *pattern library* (wakeup sources, reserved-memory lookup, NAPI on a dummy netdev, cdev class, SPI boot driver) rather than as a base, because it lost the LLI link, the SS333 controller, `SET_TX_LINK` and the framed `DL_START/FW_UPDATE` download — speculative but strongly supported by the file lists above.
- Our cbd sending 0x6f48 after a successful `BOOT_OFF` and 0x6f47 after a failure matches "clear CP boot log on success / dump it on failure"; on our 3.10 kernel both hit `default: -EINVAL` and are harmless. A port can implement them cheaply by reading/clearing 0x1FF bytes at shmem+0xC00 — but whether the SS333 CP actually writes a boot log there is unproven (offset taken from shmem-era ModAP drivers) — speculative.
- Keeping our `modem_v1_uapi.h` (0x6f19…0x6f44 plus 0x6f47/0x6f48) is consistent with the 4.9 modem_v1 ABI; adopting cpif's numbering would break our cbd (0x6f21/0x6f22 become 0x40046f21/0x40046f22 because `sizeof(struct boot_mode)` is 4 on arm32) — speculative arithmetic from the `_IOW` definition.

### Gaps
- No public changelog explains when/why LLI support was dropped; the last LLI-capable trees I found are all 3.10 (5430/5433/7420).
- I did not find any Exynos Auto tree with cpif; "Exynos Auto" as a cpif consumer is unverified.
- `CONFIG_CP_SECURE_BOOT`/SMC paths in cpif are irrelevant to SS333 (external CP, no SMC in our 3.10 driver) but I did not audit what else in cpif assumes a ModAP.

## Q3. MIPI LLI in general: mainline drivers/bindings, public spec material, Exynos LLI block

### Takeaway
MIPI LLI has no driver and no DT binding in mainline Linux (6.12 or 7.3); the only in-tree traces are the Exynos 5433 clock binding comment that CMU_CPIF clocks "LLI (Low Latency Interface) IP" and an "LLI" target label in the OMAP4/5 L3 error decoder. The spec (v2.1, Nov 2014) is members-only and the working group is hibernated; public material only covers the architecture (M-PHY + PHY adapter + data link + transaction layer, Low-Latency vs Best-Effort traffic classes, sideband signals, HIBERNATE). The most detailed public description of the Exynos LLI block is Samsung's own GPL register header.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- The Exynos register map shows the textbook LLI split: two transaction "traffic classes" (LL and BE, each with initiator/target sides and an address remap window), a service (SVC) class for remote configuration, PA-layer mount state machine, and a TL "signal" set/clear register pair used as doorbells. - The SS333 IPC rings (SBD) are explicitly derived from MIPI's "LLI_IPC_AN" (sbd.h comment), which explains why the same ring code survived onto PCIe — speculative.

## Q4. Linux wwan subsystem vs the legacy misc ABI: what an upstreamable "Samsung IPC over LLI" driver would look like

### Takeaway
The wwan core exposes control protocols as typed character-device "ports" and data as netdevs created through rtnetlink links (`IFLA_WWAN_LINK_ID`). It has precedents for vendor protocols (XMMRPC for Intel, MIPC for MediaTek, FASTBOOT, ADB, NMEA added 2024-2026) and for SoC-attached modems (rpmsg_wwan_ctrl + qcom_bam_dmux), but no "Samsung IPC/SIPC" port type, no ioctl pass-through, and no boot/flash channel — so an upstream SS333 driver would need a new port type, a redesigned boot path (devlink/firmware/remoteproc-like) and would break cbd. Keeping the legacy `umts_*` misc/cdev ABI is what every working community port does.

### Cited Findings
- [generic] Port API (6.12): `wwan_create_port(parent, type, ops, caps, drvdata)` "The port will be automatically exposed to user as a character device and attached to the right virtual WWAN device"; ops `start/stop/tx` (+ optional `tx_blocking`, `tx_poll`); RX via `wwan_port_rx(port, skb)`; flow control `wwan_port_txoff/txon` — confirmed, `ML/include/linux/wwan.h:46-128`.
- [generic] Port types in 6.12: `AT, MBIM, QMI, QCDM, FIREHOSE, XMMRPC, FASTBOOT` (`ML/include/linux/wwan.h:13-38`), names/devsuffixes table `ML/drivers/net/wwan/wwan_core.c:304-338`; `wwan_create_port` rejects `type > WWAN_PORT_MAX` (:448), so `WWAN_PORT_UNKNOWN` cannot be instantiated; the port fops' ioctl handler only implements AT/tty-style ioctls (`wwan_port_fops_at_ioctl`, :793-872) — confirmed (local).
- [generic] Mainline 7.3 adds `WWAN_PORT_ADB` and `WWAN_PORT_MIPC` ("MTK MIPC diagnostic interface"; commit 495e7c8e9601, 2024-11-07, Fibocom), `WWAN_PORT_NMEA` (5b2e294e0cd1, 2026-01-31, exported through the GNSS subsystem), and `->dtr_rts()` port op (241c9ed785d0, 2026-10-20); `WWAN_PORT_FASTBOOT` dates from e3caf184107a (2024-02-09) — confirmed, [torvalds/linux include/linux/wwan.h](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/master/include/linux/wwan.h), [495e7c8e9601](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/commit/495e7c8e9601), [5b2e294e0cd1](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/commit/5b2e294e0cd1), [241c9ed785d0](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/commit/241c9ed785d0). NMEA series history (RFC by Sergey Ryazanov June 2025, v3/v4 by Slark Xiao Dec 2025/Jan 2026) — confirmed, [LWN 1026872](https://lwn.net/Articles/1026872/), [LKML v4](https://lkml.iu.edu/hypermail/linux/kernel/2601.0/02608.html).
- [generic] Data path: `wwan_register_ops(parent, ops, ctxt, def_link_id)`; drivers implement `.newlink(ctxt, dev, if_id, extack)` and userspace creates links with `ip link add … type wwan linkid N` (`IFLA_WWAN_LINK_ID`, `ML/include/uapi/linux/wwan.h:10`; examples `ML/drivers/net/wwan/iosm/iosm_ipc_wwan.c:164,215,301`, `mhi_wwan_mbim.c:560,615,645`) — confirmed (local). `WWAN_NO_DEFAULT_LINK` lets a driver avoid creating a default netdev (`wwan.h`) — confirmed.
- [generic] SoC-integrated precedent: `RPMSG_WWAN_CTRL` "allows modems available via RPMSG channels to expose different modem protocols/ports … including AT and QMI" (`ML/drivers/net/wwan/Kconfig:77-84`; port table `rpmsg_wwan_ctrl.c:150-152`); `QCOM_BAM_DMUX` gives raw-IP netdevs (`dev->type = ARPHRD_RAWIP`, `alloc_netdev(…, "wwan%d", …)`, `qcom_bam_dmux.c:418,444`) with control over rpmsg/QRTR (Kconfig:64-72) — confirmed (local). Boot/crash of those modems is owned by remoteproc (`qcom_q6v5_mss.c:2128` `rproc_add`) — confirmed (local).
- [generic] Firmware/flash: `IOSM` selects `NET_DEVLINK` (Kconfig:95-99; `iosm_ipc_devlink.c`), MediaTek t7xx exposes `wwan0at0`, `wwan0mbim0` and a FASTBOOT port (`t7xx_port_proxy.c:65-117`) — confirmed (local).
- [other-gen, Intel] "IOSM" also exists in Samsung's tree: `config LINK_CONTROL_MSG_IOSM bool "Control Message Type: IPC Over Shared Memory (IOSM)"`, selected for `LTE_MODEM_XMM7260` (`K310/drivers/misc/modem_v1/Kconfig:32-33,95,133`); mainline `IOSM` is "IOSM Driver for Intel M.2 WWAN Device" over PCI (`ML/drivers/net/wwan/Kconfig:95-101`) — confirmed (local). Same protocol family name, different transport; wire compatibility not verified.
- [other-gen] Working community stacks keep the legacy nodes and talk SIPC/SIT/AT from userspace: ztOS (`umts_ipc0` Samsung IPC, `umts_router` AT, `rmnet0` data) and omarchy-mobile (SIT client, "ModemManager bridge", `AT+CFUN` on `/dev/umts_router`) — confirmed, [ztOS README](https://github.com/Zerone-Laboratories/ztOS/blob/main/modem/README.md), [omarchy-mobile MODEM-PLAN.md §6.1](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/blob/main/device/cheetah/MODEM-PLAN.md).

### Inferences
- **Idiomatic upstream decomposition for SS333/5433** (speculative design, built from the precedents above):
  1. `phy-exynos5433-lli` (drivers/phy/samsung): M-PHY init/CMN/OVTM tables from `exynos-mipi-lli-mphy.c`, gear set through `phy_set_mode` (UFS uses `PHY_MODE_UFS_HS_A/B`, `ML/include/linux/phy/phy.h:38-39`) or `phy_configure`.
  2. `exynos5433-mipi-lli` controller (drivers/bus or drivers/soc/samsung): clocks via CCF, PMU bit via `samsung,pmu-syscon` regmap, IRQ SPI 184/185, LL/BE remap programmed from a `memory-region`; exposes TL signals as a **mailbox controller** (doorbells, cf. mainline `drivers/mailbox/exynos-mailbox.c`) and link state (mounted/unmounted) as a callback/notifier instead of the `lli_control` sysfs command interface.
  3. `shannon-ss333` modem driver: gpiod lines + phone-active IRQ, power on/off/reset sequence, SBD/legacy ring transport, SIPC5 demux; control channels as wwan ports — which requires a new port type (e.g. "SIPC"/"SAMSUNG_IPC") following the XMMRPC/MIPC precedent — and PDP channels as wwan links (`IFLA_WWAN_LINK_ID` = PDP id, `ARPHRD_RAWIP` as bam-dmux).
  4. Boot: the framed `0x9000|id<<8` UDL protocol, NV in `/efs` and RFS service live in userspace today; upstream would want either devlink "flash"/firmware-loader style or remoteproc-style lifecycle (power, boot, crash, coredump via `umts_ramdump0`). Our cbd sequence (SET_TX_LINK, DL_START, FW_UPDATE, write(), BOOT_OFF) has no wwan equivalent.
- **Recommendation implied by the evidence**: first port keeps the 3.10 ABI exactly (misc or cdev nodes `umts_boot0/ipc0/rfs0/router/dm0/ramdump0` + `rmnet0-3`, `_IO('o', …)` numbers of `modem_v1_uapi.h`), as ztOS and omarchy-mobile did; a wwan/ModemManager front end can be a userspace bridge (omarchy-mobile's approach) or a later kernel layer. Upstreaming would be a separate, much larger redesign — speculative.
- AT traffic: if `umts_router` (SIPC ch 25, BT_DUN) speaks AT on SS333 as it does on the S9's Shannon 360 (ztOS), it is the one channel that maps 1:1 onto `WWAN_PORT_AT` — speculative for SS333.

### Gaps
- No public maintainer feedback on a Samsung IPC wwan port type exists (nothing was ever submitted, per Q2).
- Whether SS333 firmware accepts AT on `umts_router` is untested.

## Q5. Mainline Exynos 5433 support for what the modem needs (clocks, PMU, pinctrl, SPI4, GIC, reserved memory)

### Takeaway
Pinctrl banks, the SPI4 controller (at 0x14d00000, not 0x14d30000), the PMU syscon, `aclk_cpif_200`/`div_aclk_cpif_200` and the MPHY PLL are in mainline; everything LLI-specific is missing: no mipi-lli node (0x10f24000/0x10f34000/0x10fd0000, SPI 184/185), no PMU define for LLI_PHY_CONTROL (offset 0x720), and CMU_CPIF lacks all the LLI gates/muxes (`ENABLE_ACLK_CPIF`, `ENABLE_PCLK_CPIF`, `ENABLE_IP_CPIF0/1`, `SRC_SEL_CPIF1`, 150 MHz `phyclk_lli_tx0/rx0_symbol`). The 8 MiB shared region must be a `reserved-memory` node (3.10 allocated it at runtime), and the SPI4 pin group must be overridden as Samsung's board DT does.

### Cited Findings
- **CMU_CPIF (mainline)**: registers `MPHY_PLL_LOCK/CON0/CON1/FREQ_DET`, `MUX_SEL_CPIF0 0x0200`, `DIV_CPIF 0x0600`, `ENABLE_SCLK_CPIF 0x0a00` only; clocks `fout_mphy_pll`, `mout_mphy_pll`, `div_sclk_mphy`, gates `sclk_mphy_pll` (bit 9, `CLK_IGNORE_UNUSED`) and `sclk_ufs_mphy` (bit 4) — confirmed, `ML/drivers/clk/samsung/clk-exynos5433.c:839-915`; DT node `cmu_cpif: clock-controller@10fc0000`, `reg = <0x10fc0000 0x1000>` (`ML/arch/arm64/boot/dts/exynos/exynos5433.dtsi:384-391`); IDs `CLK_FOUT_MPHY_PLL 1`, `CLK_MOUT_MPHY_PLL 2`, `CLK_DIV_SCLK_MPHY 10`, `CLK_SCLK_MPHY_PLL 11`, `CLK_SCLK_UFS_MPHY 11` (`ML/include/dt-bindings/clock/exynos5433.h:191-199`) — confirmed.
- **CPIF clocks in 3.10 (needed by the LLI driver, absent upstream)**: `ENABLE_ACLK_CPIF 0x0800` bits 0-16 (`aclk_mdma0`, `aclk_lli_svc_loc/svc_rem/ll_init/be_init/ll_targ/be_targ`, `aclk_cpifnm_200` bit 7, `aclk_cpifnp_200` bit 8, `aclk_xiu_llix`, `aclk_axius_lli_ll/be`, `aclk_ppmu_llix`…; `K310/drivers/clk/samsung/clk-exynos5433.c:2416-2433`), `ENABLE_PCLK_CPIF 0x0900` (:2434-2436), `ENABLE_SCLK_CPIF` bits 0-3 (`sclk_lli_cmn_cfg/tx0_cfg/rx0_cfg`, `sclk_lli_mphy`; :1882, 2441-2443), `ENABLE_IP_CPIF0 0x0B00` gates `gate_lli_{cmn_cfg,tx0_cfg,rx0_cfg,tx0_symbol,rx0_symbol,mphy}` (bits 7-11, 15; :2526-2534), `SRC_SEL_CPIF1 0x0204` muxes `mout_phyclk_lli_tx0_symbol_user` (bit 4) / `rx0` (bit 8) (:1471-1472), fixed-rate `phyclk_lli_tx0_symbol`/`rx0_symbol` 150 MHz (:1334-1335); register offsets in `K310/arch/arm/mach-exynos/include/mach/regs-clock-exynos5433.h:160-162, 456-460` — confirmed (local). The SoC node consumes 17 of them (`K310/arch/arm/boot/dts/exynos5433.dtsi:2128-2162`; `dumps/live_fdt.dts:5508-5510`) — confirmed.
- **aclk_cpif_200** (CMU_MIF): mainline has `DIV(CLK_DIV_ACLK_CPIF_200, "div_aclk_cpif_200", "mout_aclk_mifnm_200", …)` and `GATE(CLK_ACLK_CPIF_200, "aclk_cpif_200", "div_aclk_cpif_200", …)` (`ML/drivers/clk/samsung/clk-exynos5433.c:1257, 1439`; IDs 67 and 153 in `exynos5433.h:263,346`); `div_mif_pre` exists (:1241-1243) — confirmed. 3.10 forces `dout_aclk_cpif_200` to 100 MHz at probe (dossier §1.5) — confirmed.
- **LLI↔UFS M-PHY coupling**: mainline CMU_FSYS has `FRATE(CLK_PHYCLK_LLI_MPHY_TO_UFS_PHY, …)` under "PHY clocks from LLI_PHY" and `mout_sclk_mphy` choosing between `mout_sclk_ufs_mphy_user` and `mout_phyclk_lli_mphy_to_ufs_user` (`ML/drivers/clk/samsung/clk-exynos5433.c:2025-2029, 2095-2096`) — confirmed. 3.10's M-PHY node sets `shd-refclk = <1>` "use shared reference clock for MPHY" — confirmed (`exynos5433-modem-ss333.dtsi` mphy block).
- **PMU**: mainline `pmu_system_controller: system-controller@105c0000` (`exynos5433.dtsi:915`); `ML/include/linux/soc/samsung/exynos-regs-pmu.h:355` defines offset 0x0720 only as `EXYNOS5_DPTX_PHY_CONTROL` (5250/5420 naming), no Exynos5433 LLI define; 5433 PHY-control defines nearby start at `EXYNOS5433_USBHOST30_PHY_CONTROL 0x0728` (:644) — confirmed (local). 3.10 writes `writel(1, lli->pmu_regs)` at 0x105c0720 (dossier §3.2) — confirmed.
- **Interrupts**: no mainline node uses `GIC_SPI 184`/`185` (local grep of `exynos5433.dtsi`) — confirmed. Nothing in mainline at 0x10f2xxxx/0x10f3xxxx/0x10fdxxxx; the neighbouring `pinctrl_cpif: pinctrl@10fe0000` (GIC SPI 179) exists (`exynos5433.dtsi:867-871`) — confirmed.
- **Pinctrl banks**: all present in `ML/drivers/pinctrl/samsung/pinctrl-exynos-arm64.c` 5433 tables: `gpa2` :69, `gpa3` :70, `gpf1` :71 and `gpf5` :75 (ALIVE, EINTW_EXT), `gpr2` :110, `gpr3` :111 (FSYS), `gpv7` :129, `gpc0` :131, `gpg2` :144 — confirmed (local).
- **SPI4**: mainline `spi_4: spi@14d00000`, `compatible = "samsung,exynos5433-spi"`, `GIC_SPI 412`, `dmas = <&pdma0 25>, <&pdma0 24>`, `pinctrl-0 = <&spi4_bus>`, `status = "disabled"` (`exynos5433.dtsi:1535-1553`); driver match `spi-s3c64xx.c:1658`; `controller-data { samsung,spi-feedback-delay }` binding exists (`Documentation/devicetree/bindings/spi/samsung,spi-peripheral-props.yaml:16-21`) — confirmed (local).
- **SPI4 pin override**: mainline `spi4_bus` = `"gpv7-1", "gpv7-3", "gpv7-4"`, func 3, pull-up (`ML/arch/arm64/boot/dts/exynos/exynos5433-pinctrl.dtsi:727-732`, identical to 3.10 SoC dtsi `:831-835`), but the SS333 board overrides it to `"gpv7-1", "gpv7-2", "gpv7-4"` (`K310/arch/arm/boot/dts/exynos5433-modem-ss333.dtsi:170-176`) and parks `gpv7-3` "CP_SPI_MISO" as input pull-down (`:208, :213`); board child uses `samsung,spi-chip-select-mode = <1>` (3.10-only property) and `spi-max-frequency = <100000>` — confirmed (local).
- **Reserved memory**: 3.10 allocates the 8 MiB with `memblock_alloc_base(SZ_8M, SZ_8M, MEMBLOCK_ALLOC_ANYWHERE)` at boot (dossier §1.5); our 6.12 board DT already has a `reserved-memory` node (fb, sec-log, ramoops…) but no modem region (`kernel/patches/t715-bringup/0006-ARM-dts-samsung-add-Samsung-Galaxy-Tab-S2-8.0-LTE-SM.patch:1443-1470`) — confirmed (local). cpif's convention is `memory-region` phandles on the CP node resolved with `of_reserved_mem_lookup()` ([shm_ipc.c:44-59](https://android.googlesource.com/kernel/google-modules/radio/samsung/s5300/+/refs/heads/android-gs-bluejay-android16/shm_ipc.c)); community ports mark regions `no-map` and use `memremap`/`ioremap_wc` ([omarchy-mobile §2.1](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/blob/main/device/cheetah/MODEM-PLAN.md), [ztOS README](https://github.com/Zerone-Laboratories/ztOS/blob/main/modem/README.md)) — confirmed.

### Inferences
- Minimal mainline delta for the clocks: extend `cpif_cmu_info` with `ENABLE_ACLK_CPIF`, `ENABLE_PCLK_CPIF`, `ENABLE_IP_CPIF0/1`, `SRC_SEL_CPIF1/2` registers (all inside the existing 0x1000 `reg` window) plus two `FRATE` 150 MHz PHY symbol clocks, and add new IDs to `dt-bindings/clock/exynos5433.h`; until then, a out-of-tree driver could map 0x10fc0000 itself (racy with CCF) — speculative.
- PMU access should go through the existing syscon (`regmap_write(pmu, 0x720, 1)`) instead of a 4-byte `reg` entry at 0x105c0720, which would collide with the syscon's own mapping in mainline — speculative.
- The 8 MiB region should be a dynamically placed reserved-memory node with `size = <0x800000>`, `alignment = <0x800000>` (the remap register needs power-of-two size/alignment per `exynos_lli_cal_remap`) and probably `no-map`; on our arm32 kernel it must also stay in the CP-visible window (which addresses the CP can reach through the LL/BE remap is unknown) — speculative.
- Because UFS is unused on the SM-T715 (eMMC, RADIO on `15540000.dwmmc0`), the LLI/UFS M-PHY clock coupling is a low-risk concern, but enabling the LLI M-PHY must not reparent `mout_sclk_mphy` behind CCF's back — speculative.

### Gaps
- The exact CP-visible address range for the shared region and whether the stock bootloader/TrustZone fences any DRAM for the CP are unknown (3.10 used "anywhere").
- I did not verify whether 6.12's `samsung,exynos5433-pmu` syscon covers offset 0x720 without a write filter (the mainline exynos-pmu may restrict writes on some SoCs).

## Q6. Public information about the Shannon BootROM receiving its first stage over SPI (boot_device_spi, link_device_spi, spi_boot_link)

### Takeaway
The SPI first-stage path is simple and well covered by public GPL code across generations: open a Mode-0, 8-bit SPI device and `spi_sync()` the whole BOOT image in one transfer (≤128 KiB in cpif), then wait for the CP status GPIO and (on LLI parts) for the LLI link to mount. For SS333 the board uses SPI4 @0x14d00000 at 100 kHz with a TX-only pin group; cbd drives it by switching `IOCTL_MODEM_SET_TX_LINK` to `LINKDEV_SPI`, writing the 0x29fc-byte BOOT image to `umts_boot0`, switching back to LLI and polling `lli_control` for "MIPI-LLI 1". Mainline already has every piece except a tiny SPI client driver (spidev works).

### Cited Findings
- [SS333/5433] 3.10 `link_device_spi.c`: SPI driver `.name = "spi_modem_boot"` matching `compatible = "spi_boot_link"` (:194, :203), `spi->bits_per_word = 8`, `spi->mode = SPI_MODE_0` (:137-138), whole buffer via `spi_sync` (:78); `boot_device_spi.c` (not built for this board) registers a misc device named `MODEM_BOOT_DEV_SPI` = "spi_boot_link" (:269-313; `include/linux/platform_data/modem_v1.h:476`) — confirmed (local). cbd's sequence (`ioctl 0x6f37 {3}` → one raw `write(boot_fd, BOOT, 0x29fc)` → `0x6f37 {10}` → poll `/dev/mipi-lli/lli_control` for "MIPI-LLI 1") — confirmed, `research/modem/CBD_RE.md` §6 steps 8-11 and `LOADER_SPEC.md` steps 8-11.
- [other-gen, 7420+SS333] Meizu `mipi-lli-boot-spi.c`: `modem_on()` = cp_off Hi-Z, `cp_on 0` 100 ms, `cp_reset 0` 500 ms, `cp_on 1` 100 ms, `cp_reset 1` 300 ms, optional `cp_clk_en` (:364-387) — the same order/delays as our `ss333_on`; `spi_boot_run()` = one `spi_sync` of the image, then `check_cp_status(gpio_cp_status, 100)` polling every 20 ms, then wait up to 100×20 ms for `LLI_MOUNTED` (:465-499); sysfs `boot_spi` attribute (write-only, 0200) accepts "boot"/"on"/"off" (:506-538); DT compatibles `mipi_lli_boot_spi,` and `mipi_lli_boot_spi,second` select two **embedded test loader images** `rawdata_withwhileloop[1476]` and `rawdata_withwhileloop2` (1852 bytes) compiled into the driver (:63, :347-353, :541-551); Kconfig `MIPI_LLI_BOOT_SPI` "Use SPI booting for modem" — confirmed, [m576 mipi-lli-boot-spi.c](https://github.com/meizuosc/m576/blob/master/drivers/misc/mipi-lli/mipi-lli-boot-spi.c), [m576 mipi-lli/Kconfig](https://github.com/meizuosc/m576/blob/master/drivers/misc/mipi-lli/Kconfig). The embedded blob starts with an ARM vector table and carries tags reading `VER`, `DATE`, `SIZE`, `SHA`, `BOOT` (stored byte-reversed in the array) — confirmed by reading the array header (not executed, not extracted).
- [other-gen, S5100/Pixel] cpif `boot_device_spi.c`: `compatible = "samsung,exynos-cp-spi"`, up to 2 devices, `MAX_IMAGE_SIZE SZ_128K`, image copied from userspace into `vzalloc` buffer ("MUST not enable dma-mode on SPI / dma-mode does not support non-contiguous buffer"), single `spi_sync`, `panic("CP SPI driver probe failed")` on probe error; selected by DT `cpboot_spi_bus_num` (link_device.c:3732-3740) or at runtime by `IOCTL_SET_SPI_BOOT_MODE` (bootdump_io_device.c:412-427), then reached through `IOCTL_LOAD_CP_IMAGE` (:241-247) — confirmed, [boot_device_spi.c](https://android.googlesource.com/kernel/google-modules/radio/samsung/s5300/+/refs/heads/android-gs-bluejay-android16/boot_device_spi.c). On gs201/S5300 the stock path normally lets "the ROM DMAs the BOOT image from shared memory" over PCIe instead — confirmed, [omarchy-mobile MODEM-PLAN.md §1](https://github.com/derekross/omarchy-mobile/blob/main/device/cheetah/MODEM-PLAN.md).
- [other-gen] Exynos 9820 modem_v1 also ships `boot_device_spi.c` beside `modem_ctrl_s5100.c` (S10 5G) — confirmed, [beyondlte modem_v1](https://github.com/ivanmeler/android_kernel_samsung_beyondlte).
- [other-gen, ModAP] For integrated Shannon (no SPI), cbd splits the image into BOOT and MAIN and the Secure Monitor verifies and starts the CP; TOC lists BOOT/MAIN/NV/OFFSET with load address, file offset and size; Comsecuris `shannonRE` has TOC templates and `unpack_modem.py` — likely (snippets), [Synacktiv SSTIC 2020 article](https://www.sstic.org/media/SSTIC2020/SSTIC-actes/how_to_design_a_baseband_debugger/SSTIC2020-Article-how_to_design_a_baseband_debugger-berard_fargues.pdf), [Hernandez & Muench, Emulating Samsung's Baseband](https://hernan.de/research/papers/us-20-Hernandez-Emulating-Samsungs-Baseband-For-Security-Testing.pdf). ztOS confirms the SMC-verified boot path on Exynos 9810 ("the secure monitor verifies and starts the CP") — confirmed, [ztOS README](https://github.com/Zerone-Laboratories/ztOS/blob/main/modem/README.md).
- [generic] Mainline SPI core maps `vmalloc` buffers into scatter-gather for DMA (`is_vmalloc_addr` in `spi_map_buf_attrs`, `ML/drivers/spi/spi.c:1135-1216`) — confirmed (local).

### Inferences
- For a 6.12 port the SPI stage needs no new kernel code: enable `&spi_4` with the board's pin override (gpv7-1/2/4, MISO parked), add a child at `reg = <0>`, 100 kHz, Mode 0, bound to `spidev` (or a 50-line client driver), and let the cbd replacement `write()` the 0x29fc-byte BOOT record; the `SET_TX_LINK` ioctl becomes unnecessary if userspace writes SPI directly — speculative.
- The CP side signals first-stage success through `gpio_cp_status` (gpa2-5 on our board) going high and then by mounting LLI; polling those two conditions is the minimal "boot ROM handshake" a new driver must expose (Meizu does exactly that) — speculative for SS333 on the SM-T715 (Meizu is the same CP on a 7420).
- Meizu's embedded "with while loop" images look like minimal CP-side test loaders for LLI bring-up; if they target SS333 they could test SPI + LLI mount without the RADIO image, but executing unknown CP code is a risk and their target and behaviour are unverified — speculative; not recommended without further analysis.
- cbd's single 0x29fc write is far below cpif's 128 KiB SPI limit, so the 3.10 behaviour (one `spi_sync`) is consistent across generations — speculative.

### Gaps
- No public document describes the SS333 BootROM's SPI protocol (framing, CRC/signature, max size, clock limits); everything known comes from AP-side drivers and our cbd RE.
- Why the board runs SPI4 at only 100 kHz, and whether the BootROM tolerates faster clocks, is unknown.
- The role of `gpio_cp_status` vs `phone_active` during the SPI stage on SM-T715 is inferred from the Meizu driver, not verified on our board.
